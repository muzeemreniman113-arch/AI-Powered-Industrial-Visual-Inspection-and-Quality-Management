Production Evaluation Metrics Framework

False Negative Rate (FNR) FNR = FN / (TP + FN)

In manufacturing, an undetected defect shipped to a client incurs massive recall costs and assembly line stoppage. Priority: Minimize FNR towards 0.

False Positive Rate (FPR) FPR = FP / (FP + TN)

High false alarms stop production lines unnecessarily for manual inspection, degrading factory throughput. Constraint: Keep < 1.2% in mass production.

Segmentation: IoU & Dice IoU: |G ∩ P| / |G ∪ P| • Dice: 2|G ∩ P| / (|G| + |P|)

Evaluates exact spatial localization of hairline cracks, pitting, and void regions against metallurgical ground truth.

Algorithm Supervision Levels in Factory AOI

1. Unsupervised Anomaly Detection (Zero Anomaly Prior)

Trained strictly on defect-free samples ($N$). Used when defect types are rare or unknown prior to deployment (e.g. MVTec AD, VisA, Real-IAD).

2. Weak Supervision (Image-Level Binary Flags)

Trained on image-level binary tags (Defective / Non-Defective) without bounding boxes or masks (e.g. elpv-dataset, InsPLAD-fault).

3. Strong Supervision (Pixel Masks & Bounding Boxes)

Trained with exact bounding boxes or pixel-wise segmentation masks (e.g. DeepPCB, Severstal Steel, KolektorSDD2).
